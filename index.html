Multipost

A personal tool to publish one video to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube at once.

Multipost is a small application that runs on its owner's own computer. The owner picks a video, writes the description once, chooses which of their own accounts to publish to, and Multipost sends the video to each platform through its official API (TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram API, YouTube Data API).

Multipost is not a public service: it has no servers and no user accounts of its own. It is used only by its owner to publish to accounts the owner manages.
