Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Multipost is a personal publishing tool that runs entirely on its owner's computer. This policy explains what data it handles and where that data goes.

1. Who is responsible

Multipost is developed and operated by Matthieu (mattfrb), an individual. Contact: matthieu.frb@gmail.com.

2. Data Multipost handles

  • Account connection tokens issued by TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and YouTube (Google) when the owner connects one of their accounts.
  • Basic profile information returned by those platforms: display name, username and profile picture URL, used only to show which account a video will be published to.
  • The videos and descriptions the owner chooses to publish.

Multipost does not collect any data about other people, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not show advertising.

3. Where the data is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored only in local files on the owner's computer. Videos are copied temporarily to that computer while they are being uploaded and are deleted as soon as publishing ends. Multipost has no servers and no database of its own.

4. Who the data is shared with

A video and its description are sent only to the platforms the owner selects, through their official APIs:

  • TikTok (TikTok Privacy Policy)
  • Instagram / Meta (Instagram Privacy Policy)
  • YouTube / Google (Google Privacy Policy)

Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner disconnects the account in Multipost, which deletes its tokens and profile information from the computer. Access can also be revoked at any time from each platform's settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

6. Google API Services

Multipost's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to identify the channel and upload videos to it. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

7. Children

Multipost is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top shows the latest version.
